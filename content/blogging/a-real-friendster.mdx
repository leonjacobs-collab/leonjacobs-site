A real every day hero of a man has just purchased the domain of defunct social network Friendster and guess what he is doing with it? He is reinventing social media. Sorta. 

He [forked over a total of $30'000](https://ca98am79.medium.com/i-bought-friendster-for-30k-heres-what-i-m-doing-with-it-d5e8ddb3991d) to buy the domain and related trademarks and then decided to build a new social network on it that has a small but important improvement over social networks as we got to know them. On the new Friendster, if you want to friend someone, you'd have to bump phones together. Which means you can only friend friends who are really your friends.

Such a great example of how constraints create the space for innovation.
