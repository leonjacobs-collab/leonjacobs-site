[Pippin Barr](https://pippinbarr.com/about/me/) is a professor of user experience design and [game designer](https://pippinbarr.com/games/). His [Slow As Possible](https://pippinbarr.com/as-slow-as-possible/) versions of Pong, Missile Command and Breakout will seriously mess with your brain. In a good way, I hope. Everything is slowed down, impossibly slow, that will make you smell the neurons in your brain burning down from being starved of action. A lot to process in one's reaction to this.

I love these kind of projects, like a simple reversal of a mechanic, that makes us see the the world in a completely different way.
