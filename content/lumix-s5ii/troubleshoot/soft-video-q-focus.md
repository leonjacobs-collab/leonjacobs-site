Watch the live view as you record. If the AF box is jumping around or settling on something behind your subject, that's where your softness is coming from — not the codec.

Common causes: subject detection set wrong (looking for humans when shooting a product), AF area too large, low-contrast subject, or shooting through glass/wire that the AF locks onto. *(Tree expansion in progress — file an issue if your case isn't covered.)*
