`[Focus Transition]` lets you pre-register up to **three focus positions** and smoothly rack between them during video recording with a single button press [^manual].

You set each position using manual focus, then press `[WB]`, `[ISO]`, or `[▲]` to lock them in. During recording, select position 1, 2, or 3 and the lens racks over automatically.

**Controls worth knowing about:**

- **Speed** — adjustable from `SH` (fast) to `SL` (slow)
- **Wait time** — set a delay before the transition begins, useful for getting the timing right after pressing the button
- **Focus Transition Rec** — automatically starts the transition when recording begins

This replaces the need for a follow focus system in many situations, and the transition is smoother than most humans can pull off by hand on a focus ring.
