Buried in the `[Aspect Ratio]` menu alongside the usual 3:2, 4:3, 16:9, and 1:1 are two ultra-wide panoramic ratios: **65:24** and **2:1** [^manual].

The 65:24 ratio mimics the Hasselblad XPan / Fuji TX-1 panoramic film format — a look that's become iconic in street photography and cinematic stills. At L size, you get a 6000×2208 pixel image. The 2:1 ratio gives you 6000×3000.

**Why this is more than a crop:** The camera shows you the framing live in the EVF, which fundamentally changes how you compose. You're not cropping a 3:2 frame in post — you're actually composing for the panoramic format in the moment. You can also overlay a `[Frame Marker]` to preview additional crop ratios on top.

**Restrictions:** Not available in iA mode, burst mode, time-lapse, stop motion, High Resolution mode, or with APS-C lenses [^manual].
