`[Live Cropping]` lets you set a start frame and an end frame on the full sensor area. The camera then records a 4K or FHD video that smoothly pans and/or zooms between those two frames over **20 or 40 seconds** [^manual].

The camera doesn't physically move — it's all done electronically from the oversampled sensor area. This is essentially a one-person jib/dolly move. Set the camera on a tripod and get smooth, cinematic camera movement with zero rig.

It can pan, zoom in, zoom out, or do diagonal moves — whatever path your start and end frames define. Useful for talking-head intros, product showcase shots, or any locked-off scene where you want a touch of subtle movement.
