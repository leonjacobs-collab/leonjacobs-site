In Creative Video or S&Q mode, you get a full **Waveform Monitor** and **Vector Scope** overlaid on your live view [^manual].

**The waveform** displays luminance from 0% to 109% IRE with 10% gridlines. You can reposition and resize it by dragging on screen. This is the same tool used in professional broadcast and post-production for monitoring exposure with precision — no more guessing whether you're clipping highlights or crushing shadows.

**The vector scope** shows your color distribution across R, YL, G, CY, B, and MG axes — the same tool colorists use in post-production suites to check skin tones, white balance, and color cast.

**Combined with the rest of the on-screen monitoring suite:**

- **Zebra Patterns** — two independent zebra channels with configurable base levels and ranges, including Stop-unit display when in V-Log
- **Luminance Spot Meter** — point it anywhere on screen to read IRE or Stops

You have a complete exposure and color monitoring suite without any external hardware. For V-Log work in particular, the waveform set to 42% IRE for middle gray gives you a precise exposure target [^manual].
