If you mount a manual or adapted lens that can't communicate electronically with the camera, you can register its focal length, name, and even set the **I.S. Area** (range of stabilisation) from 70% to 100% to prevent vignetting from the IBIS movement [^manual].

**What you can store:**

- Focal length (essential — without it, IBIS doesn't know what it's stabilising)
- A custom name for the lens
- I.S. Area (70%–100%, to prevent corner vignetting on lenses with smaller image circles)

You can store info for up to **12 different lenses**, switching between them from a single menu. The camera uses this data to properly drive the in-body stabilisation.

**Built-in vignetting test:** Press the d-pad to check the four corners for vignetting while the stabiliser is active. This lets you tune the I.S. Area until vignetting disappears, without trial-and-error in post.

Useful for vintage rangefinder glass on adapters, anamorphic lenses with non-standard image circles, and any "dumb" lens you'd otherwise have to leave unstabilised.
